<objective>
Surface the 07-01/07-02 engine truth in the shipped load-order/verify view
(`frontend/src/routes/+page.svelte` §4 "Deploy & verify" + §5 "Plugins & load order"),
strictly per the Phase-7 UI design contract (`07-UI-SPEC.md`). This is a PURE render layer —
every new element reflects an engine-supplied boolean or enum; the UI decides nothing about
protected/medium/reconcile status.

Four additive surfaces (UI-SPEC §1–§5):
1. **Protected-master locked rows** (SFLO-03): rows whose `protected` is true render with
   non-operable reorder ▲▼ + a disabled/checked toggle, a `PROTECTED` badge (text + `🔒`), and
   an accessible reason (aria-label/title) — the lock is reachable in the a11y tree, not
   conveyed by a bare disabled control.
2. **Medium-master badge** (SFLO-03): a `MEDIUM` badge on `medium` plugins, textually distinct
   from ESM/ESL/ESP and not accent-blue.
3. **Masterlist-age note** (SFLO-02): a muted-grey "Masterlist from {date} — may be stale" near
   Sort with LOOT (Starfield only), reading `SortProposal.masterlist_date`.
4. **SFLO-04 reconciliation** (SFLO-04): a calm in-sync line vs. an amber discrepancy list in
   the verify surface, driven by the `reconcile_plugins` `ReconcileState`.

The LOOT propose-then-apply preview is REUSED VERBATIM (already satisfies SFLO-02's
no-silent-apply — do NOT build a new one).

Purpose: make the safety guarantees visible and honest to the user without adding any decision
logic client-side.
Output: extended `api.ts` mirror types + the additive Svelte markup/CSS behind the first-launch gate.
</objective>
